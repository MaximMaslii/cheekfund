Delete your Cheekfund data

Cheekfund: Budget Tracker is developed by Maxim Maslii. Cheekfund has no user accounts: your expenses, budget, jars, wishes, IOUs and settings are stored only on your phone.

1. Delete the data on your phone

  1. Open the Cheekfund app.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all data and confirm.

All expenses, jars, wishes, IOUs, streak rewards and settings are deleted from the device immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app deletes them too.

2. Ask us to delete purchase records

If you bought a subscription or Pro Lifetime, an anonymous purchase record is kept by our payment provider RevenueCat so your purchase can be restored.

  1. Send an email to maxim.maslii777@gmail.com with the subject “Cheekfund data deletion”.
  2. Include the Google Play order number (GPA.…) from your purchase receipt, so we can find the record.
  3. We delete the record within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: all app data on your phone; the RevenueCat customer record (anonymous app user ID and purchase history).
  • Kept: Google Play keeps its own record of the payment, as required for billing, tax and accounting, under Google’s privacy policy. Your purchase stays valid: if you later tap Restore purchases in the app, it is read again from your Google Play account.
