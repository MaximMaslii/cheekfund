Frequently asked

Where is my data? Only on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers with your data.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone? Settings → Restore purchases (use the same Apple ID / Google account).

How do I cancel a subscription? iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What do Plus and Pro include? Plus (monthly): receipt scanner, unlimited jars, wishes and IOUs, any-month recaps, CSV export. Pro (yearly or lifetime): everything in Plus + the hamster wardrobe.

Auto-logging doesn’t work. iPhone: check the Shortcuts automation — Settings → Auto-logging in the app shows every step. Android: make sure notification access is on for Cheekfund and that your bank sends a notification with the amount.

How do I delete my data? Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
