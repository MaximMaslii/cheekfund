Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the Cheekfund app (“the app”) handles your information. The app is developed by Maxim Maslii, a private individual in the Republic of Moldova, who is responsible for it (the “developer”, “we”). Contact: maxim.maslii777@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • Your expenses, savings jars, wishes, debts and settings are stored only on your device.
  • We have no user accounts and no servers that store your financial data. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and the app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • The only data that leaves your device is what is needed to process purchases (via Apple, Google and RevenueCat) and to download public exchange rates.

2. Data stored on your device

Amounts, merchants, categories, notes, savings goals, wishes, debts, your monthly budget, hourly wage, names you enter for yourself and your hamster, and app settings. This data stays in the app’s private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us. It is included in your device backups (iCloud / Google) if you have them turned on; those backups are governed by Apple’s or Google’s policies.

3. Permissions and how they are used

  • Microphone and speech recognition — only while you use voice input to dictate an expense. Recognition is performed by your phone’s system speech service (Apple or Google), which may process audio under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store recordings.
  • Camera and photos — only to read a receipt that you choose to scan. Text is recognised on the device; the image is not uploaded.
  • Notification access (Android, optional) — if you turn on auto-logging, the app looks at incoming notifications on your phone and keeps only payment alerts that contain an amount with a currency. Chats, e-mail, social and call notifications are never read. Notifications containing confirmation codes, one-time passwords, 3-D Secure, CVV or PIN are discarded immediately. Card numbers, card endings and IBANs are removed on the device before anything is saved. The remaining text is kept only in a temporary on-device queue that is excluded from cloud backups, deleted as soon as the app has turned it into an expense and in any case after 7 days, and never uploaded or shared. Only the amount, currency and shop name are stored as your expense. You can turn auto-logging off in the app (the listener then keeps nothing) or revoke access in Android settings at any time.
  • Shortcuts automation (iPhone, optional) — if you set up the automation shown in the app, iOS Shortcuts opens the app with the amount and merchant of an Apple Pay payment. The app does not access Wallet or your bank.
  • Notifications — local reminders (evening reminder, wish timer). They are scheduled on the device; we do not use a push server.

4. Payment card data

Cheekfund never asks for, collects, stores or transmits payment card numbers, expiry dates, CVV/CVC codes, PINs, one-time passwords or bank login details. The app has no payment form of its own: purchases inside the app are paid through Apple or Google, which do not share your card details with us. As described above, any card or account numbers that appear in a bank notification are removed on your device before the notification is used.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple App Store / Google Play process payments. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) manages subscriptions on our behalf. It receives a random anonymous app user ID, device and app information needed for purchases, and purchase receipts/history. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Exchange rates are downloaded from the public service ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com). Like any internet request, this reveals your IP address to that service; no personal or financial data is sent.

6. Sharing initiated by you

When you share a monthly recap image or a debt reminder, it is passed to the app you choose (for example Telegram, WhatsApp or Instagram). Nothing is sent without your action.

7. Legal bases (EEA/UK users)

Processing of purchase data is necessary to perform our contract with you (providing the paid features). Optional features that use permissions rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by turning the feature or permission off.

8. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete it: Settings → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple/Google and RevenueCat as required for billing and accounting. To ask RevenueCat-related data to be deleted, email us with the request.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your financial data is only on your device, you control it directly. For anything else, write to maxim.maslii777@gmail.com.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Security

Data on the device is protected by your phone’s security (lock screen, encryption). Network requests use HTTPS.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
